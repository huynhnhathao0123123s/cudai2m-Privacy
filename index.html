1. Application Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, transmission, and protection of information by the Android mobile application Haocudai2m (package identifier: com.haocudai2m.assistant), developed and maintained by the Haocudai2m Development Team ("we", "us", or "our").

By installing, launching, or authenticating into Haocudai2m, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the practices detailed in this document. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please discontinue use of the application and uninstall it from your device.

2. Core Privacy Principles

Our engineering architecture is built around three foundational pillars of data integrity:

  • Data Minimization: We request only the minimal data and Android system permissions required to provide the core service (authenticating user identity and providing real-time question analysis).
  • Zero Data Monetization: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information, email addresses, or screen captures to data brokers, advertising networks, or third-party marketing firms.
  • Ephemeral Processing: Screen capture data processed during real-time study assistance is transient. It is streamed over secure channels directly for OCR and language model inference, and is instantly discarded from server RAM after generating the answer.

3. Categories of Data Collected & Processed

3.1. Google Account & Profile Credentials

When you initiate sign-in via the Android Credential Manager and Google Identity Services, the application requests the following non-sensitive OAuth 2.0 scopes (openid, email, profile):

  • Unique Google Subject ID (User ID): A cryptographic identifier used to establish and maintain your authenticated session.
  • Primary Email Address: Used strictly for account identification, quota management, and customer support.
  • Display Name: Rendered within the application header to personalize your user interface.
  • Profile Picture URL: Used solely to render your avatar or initials in the user profile card.

Note: Haocudai2m never accesses, requests, or stores your Google account password. All authentication handshakes are executed natively by Google Play Services.

3.2. Visual Data & Screen Captures

Haocudai2m offers a floating assistant feature that assists users in reading questions displayed on their screen. This functionality is governed by strict user consent:

  • The application only captures visual content when the user explicitly taps the floating assistant capture button after approving the Android system MediaProjection dialogue.
  • Screen captures are processed strictly within the active user session and are transmitted via encrypted HTTPS directly to the Gemini inference backend.
  • No Passive Background Recording: The application does not record your screen continuously, does not capture audio, and never takes automated captures without an explicit user touch event.

3.3. Local Device Preferences & Session State

The application saves local operational data on your device's private sandboxed storage (haocudai2m_prefs), including:

  • Authentication tokens and active login session status.
  • User interface customization settings (chosen AI reasoning model, auto-solve preferences, and overlay positioning).

4. Android OS Permissions & Justification

In accordance with Google Play Developer Program Policies, every Android permission declared in AndroidManifest.xml serves a concrete, user-facing function:

Permission Protection Level Technical Purpose & Justification
INTERNET Normal Enables outbound encrypted network communication with Google Play Services and Google Gemini API endpoints.
GET_ACCOUNTS Normal Supports legacy Credential Manager and Google Play Services account picker resolution on compatible Android versions.
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Special (Settings) Allows the floating assistant widget to hover above study applications and browser windows so users can trigger assistance without switching apps.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Normal Maintains the floating widget lifecycle and prevents the Android OS from prematurely killing the assistant while the user is actively studying.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION Dangerous (Consent) Required by Android 14+ (API 34) to capture screen content designated by the user for real-time educational OCR analysis.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime Displays the ongoing foreground service notification required by Android OS while the floating assistant is active, allowing instant termination.

5. Artificial Intelligence Processing & Third-Party Services

Haocudai2m utilizes Google Gemini API (powered by Google Cloud Platform) to process extracted question text, optical screen frames, and study prompts:

  • Inference Only: Data sent to Google Gemini is evaluated solely to return educational explanations, step-by-step reasoning, and quiz solutions.
  • No Model Training: Under Google API terms for paid/enterprise and developer console services, your private customer data and user prompts are not used to train or fine-tune public foundation models.
  • Third-Party Conformance: We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs (e.g., AdMob, Unity Ads, Facebook Audience Network) or user tracking analytics SDKs (e.g., Adjust, AppsFlyer) into the application binary.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Limited Use Disclosure: Haocudai2m's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We only use Google user data to provide or improve user-facing features that are prominent in the application's user interface.
  • We do not transfer user data to third parties unless necessary to provide or improve these features, to comply with applicable laws, or as part of a merger/acquisition with explicit notice.
  • We never use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements, including retargeting, personalized, or interest-based advertising.
  • We do not allow humans to read user data unless we have obtained your affirmative agreement, it is necessary for security investigations, or it is required to comply with law.

7. Data Retention & Erasure Policy

  • Remote Retention: None. Screen frames, OCR fragments, and generated answers are held in volatile memory only for the duration of the HTTP request cycle and are purged immediately.
  • Local Retention: Authentication state and user preferences remain stored in sandboxed app storage until you explicitly sign out or clear app storage.
  • Data Erasure: You can delete all local data at any moment by clicking the in-app Logout / Đăng xuất button or navigating to Android Settings > Apps > Haocudai2m > Storage > Clear Data.

8. Security Safeguards & Encryption Standards

We deploy robust industry-standard safeguards to protect user information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data exchanged between Haocudai2m and Google servers is enforced over TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption, preventing eavesdropping and man-in-the-middle attacks.
  • Sandboxed OS Storage: Credentials and user preferences are written with mode Context.MODE_PRIVATE, making them strictly inaccessible to other applications installed on the device.
  • Keystore Integrity: Cryptographic signatures verified via official Google Play App Signing guarantee that the APK binary has not been tampered with or modified.

9. User Rights & Account Control

Regardless of your geographic location, you retain full sovereignty over your data:

  • Right to Revoke Access: You can permanently disconnect Haocudai2m from your Google Account at any time by visiting your Google Security Dashboard at myaccount.google.com/permissions and selecting Remove Access.
  • Right to Rectification & Deletion: You can purge all cached user information from your device immediately using the built-in logout feature.
  • GDPR (European Union) & CCPA/CPRA (California) Compliance: We honor data subject rights including access, deletion, and portability. Since we do not maintain persistent remote databases of personal records, clearing your local app state completely removes your footprint.

10. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Haocudai2m is designed for general audience students in secondary, higher, and professional education. The application is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please notify us immediately so we can take steps to remove such credentials.
