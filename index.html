Privacy Policy

Haocudai2m Assistant

Last updated: October 2026 • Status: Active
Summary: Haocudai2m is an educational assistant designed to solve study questions with AI. We do not sell your personal information, do not run ads, and do not save your screen captures on remote servers.

1. What Haocudai2m Does

Haocudai2m is an Android study tool. It helps students understand and solve quiz questions by analyzing screenshots with Google Gemini AI models and returning clear explanations.

2. Data We Access and Why

Google Account Details

When you sign in with Google, we receive basic public profile information:

  • Google User ID: To keep your session active.
  • Email address: To identify your account and manage requests.
  • Display name and profile picture: To show your profile in the app header.

We do not access or store your Google password.

Screen Captures

The app includes a floating assistant button. When you tap this button, the app captures the question on your screen and sends the image securely to Google Gemini AI to generate an answer.

  • The app captures your screen only when you tap the button.
  • It never records your screen in the background.
  • Screen captures are processed in memory and are deleted immediately after the answer is delivered. We do not store your screenshots.

Local App Settings

Your chosen AI mode and session login status are saved locally on your phone so you do not have to sign in every time you open the app.

3. Android Permissions

Haocudai2m uses the following permissions solely to deliver its core features:

Permission Purpose
Internet To send questions securely to Google Gemini AI and receive explanations.
Display over other apps To display the floating assistant button while you read questions in study apps or browsers.
Screen Capture (MediaProjection) To capture the specific question area when you tap the floating assistant.
Foreground Service & Notification To keep the floating assistant ready while you study and show an active notification so you can stop it at any time.

4. Artificial Intelligence & Third-Party Processing

Question text and images are processed using the Google Gemini API. Data sent through this API is governed by Google Cloud privacy standards. Google does not use data submitted through enterprise APIs to train public AI models. We do not include any third-party advertising SDKs or tracking tools.

5. Google API Limited Use Statement

Haocudai2m's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data Storage and Deletion

  • Images: Discarded immediately after analysis. Zero remote storage.
  • Account data: Stored only on your device. Tapping Đăng xuất (Log out) in the app or clearing app data in Android Settings deletes all local login credentials immediately.
  • Revoking access: You can disconnect Haocudai2m from your Google Account at any time by visiting Google Account Permissions.

7. Security

All communication between the app and Google services uses encrypted HTTPS (TLS). Local data is kept in private app storage protected by Android's sandbox security.

8. Children's Privacy

Haocudai2m is intended for students and general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
